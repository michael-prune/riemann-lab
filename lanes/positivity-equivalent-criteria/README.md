# Positivity and Equivalent Criteria

Weil positivity, Li coefficients, Nyman–Beurling, de Branges-style routes, and
other genuine RH-equivalent criteria. Equivalence must be stated with its exact
hypotheses and source anchor.

## Active experiment

The [Xi positive geometry / Grassmannian attempt](../../experiments/positive-grassmannian/README.md)
tests the known equivalence between RH and total positivity of the completed-xi
coefficient Toeplitz matrix. Its new research target is a non-circular planar
network or Hydrotope-like carrier whose positive boundary measurements produce
that matrix uniformly.
