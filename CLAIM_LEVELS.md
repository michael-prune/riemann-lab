# Claim levels

| Level | Meaning | Does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `proposal` | A bounded candidate or question. | Evidence or novelty. |
| `source_backed` | Anchored to named sources. | Correctness or acceptance. |
| `checked_artifact` | A named deterministic artifact passed its stated check. | A general theorem. |
| `internal_theorem` | Inner Core admitted it under a pinned protocol. | External review, novelty, RH, or a Clay-prize result. |

Every finding also states assumptions, evidence, nonclaims, nearest failure
mode, and verification state. “More than two thirds of zeros” is a neighboring
zeta result and must never be described as a proof of RH.
